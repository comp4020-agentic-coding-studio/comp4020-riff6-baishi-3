# The market for authenticity

SLOP2474 — The Forger's Craft, week 11

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# An attributed work is worth a multiple of an identical, unattributed one.

Which means the incentive to forge attribution — not just the object — is
enormous, and largely legal until someone can prove otherwise.

---

## Authenticity as a question of price, not just fact

Everything so far has treated authenticity as "is this the real one." Markets
ask a different question, and the gap between the two is where most of the
real money in forgery sits.

---

## Authentication as a service industry

What auction houses, insurers and authentication services actually pay for —
pricing a claim that's much harder to verify than most financial claims.

---

## The perverse incentive

Forging paperwork is cheaper and safer than forging objects. This is exactly
why the final project asks for a provenance file convincing enough to move a
price, not just convincing enough to fool an eye.
