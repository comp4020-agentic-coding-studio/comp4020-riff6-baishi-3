# Provenance does authentication work the object itself often can't.

Which means it's just as fakeable as the object — and forging a chain of
ownership is a distinct craft from forging a canvas.

---

## Why documented history authenticates independently

An unbroken chain of ownership back to the artist's studio raises a
painting's worth over an identical one with a gap — the paper trail doesn't
change the paint, but it changes the claim.

---

## Forged provenance

Fabricated collections, faked exhibition history, and the Knoedler gallery
case — a distinct literature of scandal from forged objects.

---

## What a provenance file has to contain

- an unbroken chain of ownership
- exhibition and sale records that check out independently
- the specific places it usually breaks under scrutiny
