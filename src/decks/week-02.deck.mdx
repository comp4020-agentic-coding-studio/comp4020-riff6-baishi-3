# It isn't a sixth sense. It's a checklist run fast.

Brushwork rhythm, underdrawing, pigment chemistry against the claimed date,
wear on the frame against wear in the story.

---

## Van Meegeren and the Vermeers

The case that made the checklist explicit — a forger good enough to fool
critics, caught only once someone named what they were actually checking.

---

## What a trained eye checks, in order

- brushwork rhythm
- underdrawing
- pigment chemistry vs. claimed date
- wear pattern vs. claimed story

---

## The connoisseur's asymmetry

A forger has to get **every** item on that list right, at once, forwards.

A connoisseur only has to find the **one** item that's wrong.

That asymmetry is the shape of every detection problem this semester touches.
