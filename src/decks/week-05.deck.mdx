# Forging the hand

Text, signatures and the tell in language — SLOP2474, week 5

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# A genuine signature is fast and under-thought. A forged one is slow and careful.

The very care that makes it convincing to the eye is what makes it wrong
under a stroke analysis.

---

## Stroke dynamics

Why a forged signature is written slower than a genuine one — and why most
people can feel this the moment it's pointed out.

---

## Two forgeries that died to instruments

- the Hitler diaries: fooled a magazine and several historians
- the Hughes autobiography: fooled a publisher

Both survived human readers. Both died to a single material or stylistic
test.

---

## Stylometry, in brief

What a writer does unconsciously — word choice, rhythm, habitual error — that
a forger can't suppress even when copying deliberately.
