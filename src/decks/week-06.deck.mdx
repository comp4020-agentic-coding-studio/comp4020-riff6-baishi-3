# Every strange feature on a banknote is a scar from a specific attack.

Microprinting, colour-shifting ink, raised intaglio, uneven serial-number
kerning — read them in order and you're reading a hundred-year argument.

---

## A design history driven by forgers

- each security feature answers a prior attack
- each answer is itself attacked, eventually
- the note in your pocket is the current, temporary winner

---

## The counterfeiter's asymmetry

The state must defend **every** note in circulation.

The forger only needs **one** to pass.

---

## What this teaches about any anti-forgery system

A defence designed against a known attack only holds until the attack
changes — and the attacker always moves first. Including in a digital one.
