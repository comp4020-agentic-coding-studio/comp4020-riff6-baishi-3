description: Self-check on banknote security design and the counterfeiter's asymmetry.

questions:
  - question:
      Name two banknote security features the lecture cites as scars from
      specific historical attacks.
    answer:
      Microprinting, colour-shifting ink, raised intaglio, or uneven
      serial-number kerning (any two).
  - question: What is "the counterfeiter's asymmetry" in this lecture?
    answer:
      The state must defend every note in circulation; the forger only
      needs one note to pass.
  - question:
      What general principle about defence design does the banknote arms
      race illustrate?
    answer:
      A defence designed against a known attack only holds until the attack
      changes — the attacker always moves first.
