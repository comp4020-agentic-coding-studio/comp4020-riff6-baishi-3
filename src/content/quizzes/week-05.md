questions:
  - question:
      Why is a forged signature typically written slower than a genuine one?
    answer:
      The care that makes it convincing to the eye is the same thing that
      makes it wrong under stroke-dynamics analysis — genuine signatures are
      fast and under-thought, forged ones are slow and over-controlled.
  - question:
      What single piece of material evidence ended the Hitler diaries
      scandal, and how fast?
    answer:
      A pigment/brightener not manufactured until after the war — it ended
      the case in an afternoon.
  - question:
      What does "stylometry" analyse, per the lecture?
    answer:
      What a writer does unconsciously with language — fingerprints a forger
      can't deliberately suppress.
