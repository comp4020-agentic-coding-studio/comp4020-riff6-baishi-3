      Name two of the specific things a trained eye checks, according to the
      lecture.
    answer:
      Brushwork rhythm, underdrawing, pigment chemistry against the claimed
      date, or wear pattern against the story (any two).
  - question:
      Why does the asymmetry between forger and detector favour the
      detector?
    answer:
      A forger must get every checklist item right at once; a detector only
      has to find the one item that's wrong.
