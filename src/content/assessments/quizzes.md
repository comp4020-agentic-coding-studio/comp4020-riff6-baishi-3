## The brief

Ten short quizzes run across the semester, one released alongside each
lecture week that has real content (weeks 1–3 and 5–11). Each quiz is a
handful of questions drawn directly from that week's lecture — nothing
outside it, and nothing you couldn't answer from having watched the lecture
and skimmed the slides. Type an answer and the correct one is revealed
immediately: there's no pass/fail judgement on what you typed, just the
answer to check yourself against.

## What counts

Your best 8 quiz scores out of the 10 count toward this line; your worst 2
are dropped automatically, so there's no need to request an extension or
make up a missed week. Take the quiz any time after that week's lecture; it
stays open for the rest of semester.
